// OPERATOR

Who's behind the bench

CAK3D builds tools for the signals nobody else is looking at. Weeknights it's a soldering iron and an ESP32; weekends it's a trail with the radios running, or a laptop by the fire turning the woods into data. The work leans terminal-green and a little gamified — leaderboards, territory, badges — because the field is more fun when it's a game.

Everything here is field-tested: if it can't survive a real drive or a night in the pack, it doesn't ship.
